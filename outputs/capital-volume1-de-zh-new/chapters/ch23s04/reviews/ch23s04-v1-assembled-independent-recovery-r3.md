## Overall assessment

An ordinary undergraduate can recover the section's main line after one reading. It first distinguishes the fluid, latent, and stagnant forms of relative surplus population, then identifies pauperism as its lowest layer, derives the general relation between capital accumulation and the industrial reserve army, and finally states the opposed accumulation of wealth and poverty. The historical quotations at the end illustrate how earlier economists described or naturalized that opposition.

The Chinese names the population groups, mechanisms, comparisons, and changes of scope explicitly. Two long paragraphs impose a non-blocking memory burden (S), and one empirical/theoretical claim is difficult without further proof (A), but none makes the section's main move unrecoverable. Verdict: PASS.

## Paragraph findings

- C1: The paragraph defines the broad category through partial or complete unemployment, distinguishes cyclical eruptions from persistent forms, and announces the three persistent forms. The temporary exclusion is closed by the list “流动的、潜在的和停滞的”. No T finding.
- C2: The alternation between expulsion and renewed absorption is linked to employment growing more slowly than production, which identifies the fluid form. The conclusion follows directly from the mechanism. No T finding.
- C3: The account of fluid surplus population moves through preference for young male workers, later expulsion, migration, sector-specific immobility, rapid exhaustion of labour-power, mortality, and replacement of generations. S: the paragraph carries several connected mechanisms and two apparent contradictions at substantial length. However, “可与此同时”, “这种矛盾”, “另一个同样明显的矛盾”, “这是因为”, and “此外” identify each relation, so the paragraph remains reconstructable without an unstated bridge.
- C4: The agricultural form is explained by a one-way tendency: labour can be displaced without the compensating reabsorption found outside agriculture, leaving rural workers ready to move. The parenthetical definition of non-agricultural workers and the condition for the latent population to become fully visible remove scope ambiguity. No T finding.
- C5: The stagnant form is identified as employed but extremely insecure labour, after which the text explains its low standard of living, sources of replenishment, and intergenerational reproduction. A: the inverse relation asserted between wages/resources and family size is a strong empirical claim and is supported here mainly by analogy and quotations rather than a full demonstration. The claim itself and its role in reproducing the stagnant population are clear, so this is not a translation defect.
- C6-C9: The notes supply examples for simultaneous unemployment and labour shortage, the mortality quotation, rural-to-urban migration, and the population claim. Dates, locations, comparison groups, and quoted conclusions are identifiable. They support rather than replace bridges in C3-C5. No T finding.
- C10: The paragraph moves from relative surplus population into pauperism, explicitly excludes the lumpenproletariat for the current classification, and then enumerates three included groups. It closes by connecting pauperism back to both the active labour army and the reserve army and by naming who bears the maintenance cost. No T finding.
- C11: The accumulation law is presented as a sequence: more wealth and capital create both greater expansion capacity and more available labour; a larger reserve army means more persistent surplus and poverty; more poverty and a larger reserve army mean more officially recorded pauperism. The final qualification that circumstances change the law's concrete appearance preserves rather than erases the stated scope. No T finding.
- C12: The criticism of advising workers to regulate births follows from C11: accumulation itself continually adjusts worker numbers by creating a reserve army and then pauperism. “第一步” and “最后的结果” make the sequence explicit. No T finding.
- C13: The paragraph first states the productivity gain, then reverses the viewpoint with “不是……而是……”, and finally restates the result in literal demographic terms. The personification “劳动资料使用工人” is immediately explained by intensified competition for jobs and unstable sale of labour-power. No T finding.
- C14: The paragraph gathers the effects of productivity methods on individual workers, links surplus-value methods to accumulation, and concludes that wealth and poverty accumulate at opposite poles. Although long and rhetorically forceful, “然而”, “因此”, “最后”, and “它使” preserve the causal sequence and the referent of the final opposition. No T finding.
- C15: This transition states the purpose of the following quotations and warns that some economists confuse capitalist opposition with superficially similar pre-capitalist phenomena. The scope of the coming evidence is clear. No T finding.
- C16: Ortes, Townsend, Storch, Sismondi, and Destutt de Tracy are introduced one by one as different formulations or justifications of the wealth-poverty opposition. S: the paragraph is exceptionally long and shifts among five speakers plus the narrator's irony; shorter author-by-author paragraphs would reduce memory load. Each shift nevertheless names the new speaker, and the repeated topic remains stable, so no T arises.
- C17-C22: These notes document the wealth/poverty opposition and identify or expand the authors cited in C16. The long Townsend note clearly separates Stewart's view from Townsend's conclusion, while the shorter notes have unambiguous anchors. No T finding.

## Failure probes

- Main line: The progression is three normal forms of surplus population, the lowest pauperized layer, the general accumulation law, its implication for worker-directed population advice, the productivity mechanism, and the wealth-poverty opposition. The historical material is announced as evidence about that last opposition. Result: no T.
- First-use terminology: “相对过剩人口” is defined through employment status; “流动”, “潜在”, and “停滞” are each explained through a concrete labour-market mechanism; “赤贫” is classified by groups; “产业后备军” is made legible by its contrast with “现役劳动军” and by repeated descriptions of labour available for expansion. Result: no T.
- Referents: “它”, “这里”, “这部分人”, “这种矛盾”, “这批人”, “他们”, “这项费用”, and “这种对立” all have recoverable local antecedents. In the long quotation paragraph, every new view is attached to a named author before pronouns resume. Result: no T.
- Connectors and actual relations: “因此”, “可与此同时”, “这是因为”, “此外”, “所以”, “可是”, “正因为如此”, “同时”, “由此”, “换句话说”, and “然而” consistently mark consequence, contrast, cause, addition, reformulation, or qualification. None is doing the work of a missing indispensable premise within the section's stated argument. Result: no T.
- Scope and comparisons: Cyclical surplus forms are temporarily set aside before the three persistent forms are listed; agriculture is compared with non-agricultural sectors only on displacement and reabsorption; pauperism temporarily excludes the lumpenproletariat before listing the included groups; life expectancy, rural/urban growth, reserve/active armies, and wealth/poverty poles all identify both sides of the comparison. Result: no T.
- Paragraph structure: C3, C5, C14, C16, and C19 are long, but each keeps a stable question or marked sequence. The two S findings concern reading load rather than a broken argument. Notes remain ancillary and do not conceal a bridge absent from the main prose. Result: no T.
