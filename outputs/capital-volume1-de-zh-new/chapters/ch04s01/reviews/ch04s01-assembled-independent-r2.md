## Overall assessment

An ordinary undergraduate can recover the section's main line after one reading. It begins with money as capital's first form, contrasts commodity-money-commodity with money-commodity-more money, derives surplus value and the endless character of valorization, then explains capital as value moving through money and commodity forms before returning to the general formula. The numerical examples repeatedly anchor the abstractions. No passage makes the actor, direction of exchange, comparison, or conclusion unrecoverable.

The section contains two non-blocking wording or presentation risks (S) and one authorial conceptual difficulty (A). None is an unresolved T defect, so the verdict is PASS.

## Paragraph findings

- C1-C6: The opening establishes commodity circulation as the historical starting point, brackets use-values in order to inspect the economic form, and explains why new capital first appears as money. C4 states the question that C5 answers by setting the two circulation routes side by side. The footnote contrast between land and money is readable as supporting historical context. No T finding.
- C7-C12: The purchase and sale stages of G-W-G are explicitly unpacked, followed by a concrete 100/110-pound example and a clean common-feature/difference comparison. S: the prose first writes the formulas in Chinese words and then changes to G and W without a separate legend. C7 immediately defines G-W as money becoming a commodity and W-G as the reverse, so the notation is recoverable on first encounter and does not block the argument.
- C13-C18: The text distinguishes money that is spent from money that is advanced, then follows whether the same money or the same commodity changes hands. The return-to-origin test is made concrete with wheat and clothing. The contrast between consumption/use-value and return of exchange-value is explicit. No T finding.
- C19-C21: The section explains why equal endpoints are meaningful in W-G-W but empty in G-W-G, then introduces G-prime, delta G, and surplus value with a worked numerical example. The claim that valorization has no internal endpoint follows visibly from the fact that both the initial and enlarged sums remain finite and can restart the same cycle. No T finding.
- C22: The capitalist's subjective aim is tied to the objective movement of valorization, and the contrast with the hoarder is signalled by “不过” and “却”. S: “贮藏货币的人试图把钱从流通中救出来，以此不断增加价值” can momentarily sound as if withdrawing one sum makes that sum self-expand, although “试图”, the earlier statement that hoarded money does not add a cent, and the following contrast with repeated circulation make the intended hoarding strategy recoverable. This does not overturn the paragraph's main distinction.
- C23: Money and commodities are presented as alternating forms of value, and the passage moves from the provisional formulas “capital is money/commodity” to the fuller claim that value is the subject of a self-expanding process. A: treating value as an apparently self-moving subject is conceptually difficult and deliberately personified, but the preceding form changes and the separation of original value from surplus value state what the claim means. The difficulty is not caused by an omitted bridge.
- C24-C33: The notes supply quotations and historical commentary on money exchange, profit re-entering capital, household management versus money-making, the capitalist's aim, hoarding, and definitions of capital. The long Aristotle note has its own visible contrast and endpoint. None introduces an unresolved referent that is required for the main text. No T finding.
- C34-C36: These paragraphs restate value's movement in ordinary words, explain why money is required as an independent form but is not capital by itself, and return to money-prime as the compressed movement. The father/son analogy is rhetorically unusual but its mapping to 100 plus 10 equals 110 is explicit. No T finding.
- C37-C39: Commercial, industrial, and interest-bearing capital are compared by showing how the middle steps expand or disappear while the route from money to more money remains. The final one-sentence conclusion accurately closes the announced task, with the last footnote reinforcing rather than replacing it. No T finding.
